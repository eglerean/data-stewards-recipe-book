# Tom Kha Gai (Thai Coconut Chicken Soup)

## Ingredients

- 400 ml coconut milk  
- 300 ml chicken broth  
- 2 chicken breasts (thinly sliced)  
- 3–4 slices galangal (or ginger as substitute)  
- 2 stalks lemongrass (cut into pieces and lightly crushed)  
- 3–5 kaffir lime leaves (torn)  
- 150 g mushrooms (e.g., champignons or oyster mushrooms)  
- 1–2 Thai chilies (smashed, optional for heat)  
- 2–3 tbsp fish sauce  
- 1–2 tsp sugar (palm sugar preferred)  
- Juice of 1 lime  
- Fresh cilantro (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. In a pot, bring the chicken broth to a gentle simmer.  
2. Add galangal, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves, and chilies. Let it simmer for 5–10 minutes to infuse the flavors.  
3. Pour in the coconut milk and stir gently. Keep the heat medium-low (do not boil vigorously).  
4. Add the sliced chicken and cook for about 5–7 minutes until fully cooked.  
5. Add mushrooms and simmer for another 3–5 minutes until tender.  
6. Season the soup with fish sauce and sugar. Taste and adjust as needed.  
7. Turn off the heat and stir in lime juice.  
8. Remove large herbs (lemongrass, galangal, lime leaves) if desired.  
9. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro.
