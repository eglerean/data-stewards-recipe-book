# Nakit & muusi (sausages & mash)

## Ingredients

- 1 pack HK Aitonakki™ Amerikan Pekoni® (whole sausages)
- 2 onions
- salt
- butter and rapeseed (canola) oil for frying

### Brown sauce
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp plain (wheat) flour
- 5 dl mild beef stock
- salt
- freshly cracked black pepper

### Mashed potatoes
- 500 g floury potatoes (e.g., Puikula)
- 100 g butter
- a splash of milk
- a pinch of salt

## Directions

### Mashed potatoes
Peel and boil the potatoes until fully cooked. Drain well.
Beat the potatoes with the butter and a splash of milk until smooth with an electric mixer. Season with salt if needed.

### Brown sauce
Melt the butter in a saucepan.
Add the flour and brown it, stirring constantly with a spatula until the flour turns dark brown (take care, patience is key).
Gradually pour in the beef stock while stirring vigorously.
Bring to a brief boil while stirring, then let the sauce simmer gently for about 15 minutes.
Season with cracked black pepper and salt if needed.
Onions and sausages
Slice the onions and fry the slices in a knob of butter until nicely golden brown. Season with salt and cracked black pepper.
Fry the sausages in a butter–rapeseed oil mixture until golden on both sides.
Serve the sausages with the mashed potatoes, brown sauce, fried onions, and sliced pickles.
